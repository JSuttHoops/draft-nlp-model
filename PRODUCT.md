# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Basketball analysts, scouts, draft writers, and model builders who need to interrogate how scouting language connects to NBA draft outcomes. They are comparing historical outcomes, current-class prospects, model scores, trait flags, and sentence-level evidence in one place.

## Product Purpose

Draft Language Lab turns the NBA draft NLP/LLM handoff package into a usable one-page explorer. It helps users select trait families and signal types, see what historically happened to matching players, inspect prospective 2026 profiles, and open a player to read the scouting phrases behind the model signals.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, film-room precise. The product should feel like a serious analyst's notebook with enough landing-page polish to invite exploration, without feeling like a betting product or a generic dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid fantasy sports, sportsbook, dark neon analytics, template SaaS hero pages, oversized KPI walls, cluttered CRM tables, and pick-vs-mock-pick comparison framing. The UI should not overclaim the model as an oracle.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence close to the score.
- Let the real dataset fields drive the interface.
- Keep the first screen inviting, then get directly to the data.
- Treat combo flags as interpretable warnings, not magic predictors.
- Use restrained product affordances so analysts can scan, filter, and compare quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and color-independent labels for positive, negative, caveat, and neutral signals.
