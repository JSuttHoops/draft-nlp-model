## Risk Combos

The combo signals are stronger than the single-trait signals.

Historical mature, below expected:

| Risk combo | Below expected rate | Relative lift |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upside/tools + Injury/medical | 64.0% | +20.1% |
| Creation/Handle + Feel/IQ | 58.9% | +10.5% |
| Defense + Upside/tools | 58.8% | +10.4% |
| Shooting + Upside/tools | 58.2% | +9.2% |
| Feel/IQ + Shooting | 58.0% | +8.7% |

Recent 2022-25 actual-to-date, below expected:

| Risk combo | Below expected rate | Relative lift |
|---|---:|---:|
| Finishing + Upside/tools | 76.9% | +44.3% |
| Creation/Handle + Size/frame | 75.0% | +40.7% |
| Upside/tools + Size/frame | 72.7% | +36.5% |
| Feel/IQ + Size/frame | 68.4% | +28.4% |
| Creation/Handle + Finishing | 65.5% | +22.9% |

Interpretation: single positive traits are modest upside nudges. Concern language and especially concern combinations are more useful as downside-risk signals than as clean star-finding signals.
